# Portfolio Evidence Checklist

Use this page to keep the public portfolio accurate and interview-ready.

## Evidence to include
- [ ] Final Excel analysis workbook with PivotTables and management summary
- [ ] Prepared dataset used by the analysis
- [ ] Power BI PBIX report
- [ ] Dashboard screenshots (overview and useful drill-downs)
- [ ] Confirmed KPI values and at least three evidence-based findings
- [ ] Short explanation of data-quality actions and validation

## Quality checks
- [ ] Confirm final row count after cleaning
- [ ] Reconcile Excel and Power BI totals
- [ ] Verify distinct shipment count
- [ ] Validate Late Shipment Rate numerator and denominator
- [ ] Check filtered KPI behavior and slicers
- [ ] Remove personal or confidential information from all files

## Interview preparation
Be ready to explain:
1. The business problem and the dataset's record grain.
2. The data-quality issues identified and how they were handled.
3. Why each Excel analysis or visual was selected.
4. How the DAX measures work, especially filtered KPIs.
5. What the evidence says, what it does not say, and what action management could take.

**Accuracy rule:** Do not publish guessed figures, unsupported impact claims, or findings that cannot be traced to the workbook or dashboard.
