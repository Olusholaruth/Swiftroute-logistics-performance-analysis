# SwiftRoute Logistics | Operations & Financial Performance Analysis

An end-to-end **Excel and Power BI business analytics capstone** built around a Nigerian logistics case study. The project moves from raw shipment data through data preparation and business analysis to an interactive management dashboard.

> **Portfolio context:** SwiftRoute Logistics is a simulated business case supplied for an analytics capstone. This project is not employment or consulting work for a real SwiftRoute company.

## Project at a glance

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Raw dataset | 100,000 shipment records |
| Prepared dataset | 98,000 records |
| Dataset fields | 20 columns |
| Main tools | Microsoft Excel, Power Query, Power BI |
| Analysis focus | Revenue, profit, shipments, delivery outcomes, delays, carriers, services and regions |
| Deliverables | Excel analysis workbook, Power BI report, business insights and recommendations |

## Business problem

Management needs a reliable view of revenue, profitability, shipment volume, delivery performance and operational delays across locations, carriers, service types and package categories. The analysis is intended to help management identify performance patterns and areas that merit further investigation.

## Business questions addressed

- Which destination region generates the highest revenue?
- How do revenue and profit vary by package category, carrier, month, customer segment and service type?
- How many shipments are delivered to Lagos, and what revenue do they generate?
- What is the profit generated by shipments delivered within the South West region?
- What is the average recorded delay for Express shipments?
- Where are delay days concentrated, and how should delay totals be interpreted alongside shipment volume?
- How do delivery outcomes and service types compare?

The supplied capstone brief also specifies Power BI analysis of revenue and profit trends, carrier delivery performance, delivery status, top destination cities, revenue versus profit, customer shipment volume, distance versus delivery performance and interactive filtering.

## Data preparation

The source brief identifies deliberate data-quality issues involving duplicate records, inconsistent categorical formatting and unsuitable data types. The workbook contains separate raw and cleaned data worksheets:

- Raw worksheet: 100,000 rows (including the header)
- Cleaned worksheet: 98,000 rows (including the header)
- 20 columns in each worksheet

The project workbook also contains aggregation questions, a summary analysis and PivotTable outputs. The cleaning and validation approach should be reviewed alongside the workbook before interpreting the results.

## Tools and techniques

- **Excel:** PivotTables, conditional calculations, aggregation and management summary.
- **Power Query:** data preparation and transformation.
- **Power BI:** interactive dashboard and DAX measures using filter context.

SQL and Python are not presented as tools used in this project.

## Selected results from the Excel workbook

| Analysis | Result |
|---|---:|
| Highest-revenue destination region | South West — ₦401,356,997.96 |
| Next-highest destination region | North Central — ₦265,141,566.11 |
| Lagos shipment revenue | ₦230,405,681.69 |
| Lagos shipment volume | 19,637 |
| South West total profit | ₦172,319,276.64 |
| Express average delay | 0.8865 days (approximately 0.89 days) |
| Highest-revenue customer segment | Retail — ₦663,484,334.70 |
| Largest customer segment by shipment volume | Retail — 56,877 shipments |
| Highest average revenue and profit per shipment by service | Same Day |

**Interpretation note:** A region's total delay days should be interpreted alongside shipment volume. A larger total alone does not establish that the region has the poorest delivery performance.

## Management observations

1. **South West leads regional revenue** at approximately ₦401.36 million. Management could investigate the commercial and operational factors behind this result before deciding whether practices can be replicated elsewhere.
2. **Retail is the largest customer segment** by both revenue (approximately ₦663.48 million) and shipment volume (56,877). Maintaining service quality for this segment is commercially important.
3. **Express shipments average approximately 0.89 delay days.** The workbook summary identifies this as the lowest average delay among the service types; the operational processes supporting this result may be worth examining.
4. **Same Day has the highest average revenue and average profit per shipment** in the workbook's service comparison. This is an average-per-shipment comparison, not proof that it has the highest total contribution.

These observations describe the supplied dataset. They do not establish causation.
