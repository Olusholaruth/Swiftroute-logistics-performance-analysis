# Business Questions and Project Requirements

This project follows the supplied *SwiftRoute Logistics — Portfolio-Ready Capstone Project* brief.

## Business case

SwiftRoute Logistics is presented as a Nigerian logistics and delivery company. Management needs clearer reporting on revenue, profitability, delivery performance, delays and operational efficiency.

## Business objectives

1. Evaluate revenue and profitability across locations, services, carriers and package categories.
2. Understand shipment volume and delivery performance.
3. Identify locations and operational areas experiencing significant delays.
4. Compare carrier and service-type performance.
5. Provide management with a clear, interactive view of the business.

## Excel business requests

- Regional revenue and highest-revenue region
- Revenue, profit and shipment count by package category
- Shipment count, revenue, profit and delay days by carrier
- Monthly revenue, profit and shipment volume
- Lagos shipment revenue
- South West shipment profit
- Average delay for Express shipments
- Lagos shipment volume
- Revenue by customer segment
- Average revenue, shipping cost, profit and shipment volume by service type
- Delay days by destination region, interpreted with shipment volume
- A concise management summary with evidence-based findings

## Power BI requirements

The brief requires eight KPI cards: Total Revenue, Total Profit, Total Shipments, Total Delay Days, Late Shipment Rate, Lagos Revenue, Lagos Delayed Shipments and South West Delay Days.

Required analysis includes revenue and profit trends, revenue by destination region, profit by package category, carrier revenue and delivery performance, delivery status, regional delays, service performance, top destination cities, revenue versus profit, shipment volume by customer type, distance versus delivery performance, interactive slicers and a management insight view.

The brief asks for at least three filtered KPI measures to use `CALCULATE()` appropriately.

## Final deliverables

- Prepared dataset
- Excel workbook with business analyses, PivotTables, conditional calculations and management summary
- Power BI report
- At least three evidence-based business insights and practical recommendations
- Explanation of AI use and validation where relevant
