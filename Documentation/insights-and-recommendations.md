# Management Insights and Recommendations

The following observations are supported by the supplied Excel workbook.

## 1. Regional revenue concentration

The South West generated the highest regional revenue at **₦401,356,997.96**, followed by North Central at **₦265,141,566.11**.

**Recommendation:** Investigate the customer mix, shipment volume, carrier coverage and service mix contributing to South West revenue. Test whether any successful practices can be adapted in lower-revenue regions rather than assuming the same drivers apply everywhere.

## 2. Retail customer segment

Retail generated **₦663,484,334.70** in revenue and accounted for **56,877 shipments**, the largest shipment volume among the customer segments.

**Recommendation:** Protect service quality for this high-volume segment. Monitor its revenue, delivery outcomes and delay performance together.

## 3. Express delivery delays

The workbook reports an average Express delay of **0.8865 days**, approximately **0.89 days**. The workbook's management summary identifies Express as having the lowest average delay among the service categories.

**Recommendation:** Review the processes supporting Express performance and assess whether relevant practices can reduce delays in Standard and other services. Validate the comparison against the same delay definition and period.

## 4. Service economics

Same Day has the highest average revenue (**₦17,162.61**) and average profit (**₦7,359.29**) per shipment in the workbook's service comparison.

**Recommendation:** Evaluate Same Day unit economics alongside its shipment volume (11,812) and operational capacity. Higher averages do not by themselves prove the highest total profit contribution.

## Limitations

These are descriptive findings from a simulated capstone dataset. They do not establish why a result occurred or prove that a recommendation will produce a particular outcome. Additional operational context would be required for causal conclusions.
